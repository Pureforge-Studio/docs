## How to Get Help
- **Support Ticket & Bug Tracker:** Open an issue on our **[GitHub Support Tracker](https://github.com/Pureforge-Studio/docs/issues)**
- **Support Email:** `uo7vhd510@mozmail.com`
- **Target Response Time:** 1–2 business days (Monday–Friday).

## Information to Include in a Support Request
To help us resolve your question as quickly as possible, please include:
1. Which application you are using (*Mermaid Studio*, *Markdown Tree Studio*, or *Bulk Label Governance*).
2. Whether you are using **Confluence Cloud** or **Jira Cloud**.
3. The status banner message shown at the top of the app (*Amber Refusal Diagnostic* or *Red Fault Code*), if applicable.
   *(Never include sensitive company data or credentials in a support ticket.)*
